About the class

Strength training, guided by a physical therapist

This isn't a typical gym class. Every session pairs a board-certified physical therapist from Mend with an experienced strength coach, so you can lift with confidence — whether you're brand new, returning after an injury, or working around aches and pains.

Two coaches, every class

A physical therapist and a strength coach watch your form and adjust each movement for you.

Strength + intervals

Warm up together, build strength with weights, then finish with short, high-intensity intervals.

Small groups

Classes are capped at 20, so everyone gets real coaching and personal feedback.

Built for women's health

Training that supports bone density, heart and metabolic health, and the menopause transition.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my first class really free?

Yes. Your first Women's Strength class is completely free — no payment info and no commitment. It's one free class per person.

I've never lifted weights. Is this class for me?

Absolutely. Our classes include women of every age, ability and body type, and plenty of them had never lifted before their first class. Your coaches teach every movement and scale it to you.

I have an injury or chronic pain. Can I still join?

Yes — that's exactly why a physical therapist coaches every class. Tell us what's going on in your intake form and your coaches will adapt the movements for you.

Prefer to talk it through first? You can book a visit with Mend at (720) 772-6780.

What should I bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, athletic shoes and a water bottle. All the equipment is provided.

What happens when I arrive?

Come 5–10 minutes early and introduce yourself to the coaches — they'll know you're coming. They'll have you sign a waiver, show you around the gym and get you set up for class.

How big are the classes?

Classes are capped at 20 people with two coaches, so everyone gets attention and personal feedback.

How do I change or cancel my reservation?

To change your class, come back to this page and reserve a new time with the same email — it replaces your current reservation. To cancel, just reply to your confirmation email.

What happens after my free class?

If you love it, choose a plan that fits your week — see pricing. We recommend two classes a week for the best results.
